1. Purpose and Scope
This document defines a generic Field Service Management (FSM) and Warehouse Management System (WMS) platform for service-based organizations. It is not designed specifically for banks. Any organization can onboard customers, customer sites, assets/devices/equipment, service contracts and field engineers, then execute installation, preventive maintenance, breakdown/service, replacement, de-installation and reverse-logistics workflows.
Support multiple clients/customers, industries, branches, regions, service locations and warehouses.
Track serialized and non-serialized inventory from procurement through customer installation and return.
Treat the Field Service Engineer (FSE) as a controlled mobile inventory holder/virtual stock location.
Provide configurable work-order types, forms, checklists, evidence requirements, statuses and SLA rules.
Preserve complete chain-of-custody and audit history for every serialized asset.
Support offline-first mobile execution with barcode/QR scanning, GPS, photos, signature and OTP.

7. Complete WMS Process
7.1 Procurement and Inbound
Purchase/expected receipt is created or imported from ERP/procurement system.
Warehouse creates/receives ASN containing vendor, SKU/model, quantity and expected serialized items where available.
Vehicle/package receipt is recorded and GRN is initiated.
Items are counted and serialized identifiers such as serial number/IMEI/asset tag are scanned.
Inbound QC records accepted, damaged, short, excess or rejected quantity/serials.
Accepted stock is assigned to receiving/staging location.
Putaway task recommends storage bin based on warehouse rules.
Operator scans source, destination bin and serial; putaway is confirmed.
Inventory becomes AVAILABLE at the confirmed warehouse/bin.
Rejected/damaged items move to quarantine/rejection location and remain blocked from allocation.
Inbound Document / Transaction
Key Fields
ASN
asn_no, vendor, warehouse, expected_date, SKU/model, qty, serial list, source reference
GRN
grn_no, ASN, received qty, accepted/rejected qty, receiver, timestamp
Inbound QC
serial/SKU, condition, QC status, defect/reason, evidence
Putaway
task_id, source location, destination bin, serial/SKU, qty, operator, time

7.2 Inventory Storage and Control
Inventory is maintained by location + SKU/model + serial (when serialized) + status + condition.
Serialized stock has exactly one current holder/location at a time.
Supported stock states include AVAILABLE, RESERVED, IN_TRANSIT, ISSUED_TO_ENGINEER, INSTALLED, RETURNED, QUARANTINE, REPAIR, REFURBISHED and SCRAP.
Cycle count and stock reconciliation must support serial scanning and variance approval.
Inventory adjustments require privileged permission, reason code and immutable audit.
7.3 Warehouse / Hub Transfer
Source warehouse creates Stock Transfer Order to another warehouse, region, hub or authorized engineer location.
Required stock is reserved.
Picklist is generated by bin/location.
Picker scans bin and serial/SKU; system validates reservation.
Packing/dispatch is prepared with transfer document.
Dispatch confirmation creates source OUT movement and destination expected receipt/auto-ASN.
Stock status becomes IN_TRANSIT.
Destination scans transfer and received serials.
Short/excess/damaged discrepancies enter exception workflow.
Receipt confirmation creates destination inventory and completes transfer.
7.4 Region/Hub → Field Engineer Issue
Coordinator/stock manager reserves an asset/component against a work order or engineer.
Region stock manager opens FSE Issue transaction.
Asset/device/component is scanned.
System validates availability, model compatibility, customer ownership rules and reservation.
Engineer scans/accepts physical receipt in mobile app.
Inventory moves Region/Hub → FSE virtual inventory.
Serialized asset status becomes ISSUED_TO_ENGINEER.
Issued stock appears in engineer Stock-in-Hand with issue date and aging.
7.5 Engineer → Customer Site Installation
Engineer opens assigned Installation Work Order.
At site, engineer performs GPS check-in and verifies customer/site.
Engineer scans asset from Stock-in-Hand.
Backend validates serial, engineer possession, work-order allocation and model compatibility.
Required components/accessories/consumables are scanned/recorded.
Installation, configuration and tests are completed.
Mandatory evidence/checklist and customer signature/OTP are captured.
On successful submit, inventory movement FSE → Customer Site is created.
Asset-to-Site assignment becomes active and asset status becomes INSTALLED.
Consumed non-returnable materials reduce engineer inventory.
7.6 Service / Maintenance Inventory
Service work may consume components/consumables from engineer inventory.
Removed defective components can be captured as returnable stock with condition.
Asset remains INSTALLED when successfully repaired at site.
If repair requires workshop movement, asset can move Customer Site → FSE → Hub/Warehouse → Repair Centre.
Every part consumption and removed-part return is linked to the work order.
7.7 Replacement Process
Replacement asset is reserved and issued to engineer.
Engineer scans old installed asset and replacement asset.
Old asset condition, reason and evidence are captured.
System atomically unassigns old asset and assigns new asset to customer site.
Old asset moves Customer Site → FSE and becomes RETURNED_TO_ENGINEER.
New asset moves FSE → Customer Site and becomes INSTALLED.
Both serials remain linked to the same replacement work order.
Old asset enters reverse logistics for inspection/repair/refurbish/scrap.
7.8 De-installation and Reverse Logistics
Engineer verifies and scans installed asset at customer site.
Asset-to-site assignment is closed/unassigned.
Condition, accessories returned, photos and customer confirmation are captured.
Asset moves Customer Site → FSE.
Engineer returns asset to Region/Hub using scan-based handover.
Region/Hub confirms receipt and records condition/discrepancy.
Asset may be consolidated and transferred Region/Hub → Central Warehouse or Repair Centre.
Warehouse/repair QC determines Available, Repair, Refurbish, Quarantine or Scrap disposition.

8. Work Order / FSM Process
Service request is created manually, by customer portal/API, call centre, email integration or scheduled preventive-maintenance engine.
System validates customer, site, contract/SLA, serviceability and work-order type.
Work order is routed to region/dispatcher.
Coordinator assigns engineer based on skill, location, workload, availability and SLA.
Required asset/parts are reserved and allocated.
Engineer receives task on mobile and accepts/rejects according to policy.
Engineer travels, checks in, executes configured workflow, captures evidence and obtains customer confirmation.
Work order becomes COMPLETED or QC_PENDING.
Back-office/customer QC can approve, reject/reopen or close.
Inventory and asset lifecycle transactions remain linked to the work order.

9. Work Order Status Lifecycle
CREATED → VALIDATED → ASSIGNED → ASSET/PARTS_ALLOCATED → ACCEPTED → EN_ROUTE → CHECKED_IN → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED → QC_PENDING → CLOSED
Alternate controlled states: PENDING, ON_HOLD, REJECTED, CANCELLED, REOPENED. Transition rules are server-controlled and configurable.
10. Asset Status Lifecycle
RECEIVED → AVAILABLE → RESERVED → IN_TRANSIT → IN_REGION/HUB → ISSUED_TO_ENGINEER → INSTALLED → UNDER_SERVICE → RETURNED_TO_ENGINEER → IN_TRANSIT_RETURN → RETURNED_WAREHOUSE → REPAIR → REFURBISHED → AVAILABLE, or SCRAP.

18. Key Acceptance Criteria
No bank-specific field is mandatory in the core data model; industry/customer-specific identifiers use configurable external references.
Every serialized asset has one traceable current location/holder and complete movement history.
An engineer cannot install an asset not held/allocated to them unless an authorized business rule permits it.
Replacement updates old-return and new-install movements atomically.
De-installed/returned assets remain in engineer custody until receiving location confirms handover.
Offline retries do not duplicate work-order events or inventory movements.
Mandatory checklist/evidence/customer confirmation blocks completion when configured.
Customer/site/asset/service history is visible end-to-end.
All inventory adjustments, overrides and mapping corrections are audited.
WMS and FSM remain synchronized through shared asset IDs, work-order IDs and movement source references.
19. Recommended Technical Architecture
Web Portals + React Native/Flutter Mobile → API Gateway → Authentication/RBAC → Customer/Contract Service + Work Order/FSM Service + WMS/Inventory Service + Asset Service + Evidence Service + Repair/RMA Service + Integration Service → Relational Database + Object Storage + Cache/Queue. A modular-monolith deployment is suitable initially, with clear domain boundaries for future service extraction.
20. Developer Rules
Treat Customer, Customer Site, Asset, Work Order and Inventory Movement as first-class domain entities.
Never infer inventory solely from work-order status; inventory movements are explicit transactions.
Keep asset status separate from current physical location/holder.
Use append-only serialized movement history.
Make workflows, checklists, evidence and external reference fields configurable by customer/work-order/model.
Use scan-first UX for serialized assets/components.
Use server-controlled transition matrices; mobile/web clients request commands, not arbitrary statuses.
Use UTC server timestamps and retain captured local/mobile timestamp for offline events.
Version checklist/templates so historical jobs remain reproducible.
Maintain complete customer-site service history independent of engineer reassignment.

23. Revised Inbound Process - Master Carton and Inner Carton
ASN is created with vendor, warehouse, product/SKU, expected quantity and packaging details.
Vehicle/shipment arrives at receiving dock and ASN is selected or scanned.
Receiver scans the Master Carton barcode.
System validates that the Master Carton belongs to the ASN and is not already received.
System loads the expected Inner Cartons and expected product quantity for that Master Carton.
Receiver scans each Inner Carton barcode inside the Master Carton, or uses trusted pre-advice hierarchy when configured.
For serialized products, serial number/IMEI is scanned or validated against each Inner Carton.
System validates Product Master packing configuration: product, units per inner carton, inner cartons per master carton and total quantity.
Short, excess, duplicate, wrong-SKU, wrong-carton or damaged conditions are moved to exception handling.
GRN is generated/confirmed for accepted quantity and serialized content.
Inbound QC is performed at Master Carton, Inner Carton and/or serial level according to product policy.
Accepted handling units move to receiving/staging inventory and become eligible for putaway.
Rejected or damaged handling units move to quarantine/rejection location and cannot be allocated.

24. Putaway Process - Carton-Aware Design
Putaway shall be handling-unit aware. The warehouse may put away an unopened Master Carton, an individual Inner Carton, or loose serialized/non-serialized product after a carton has been broken. The WMS must preserve the parent-child hierarchy and prevent double movement of both a parent carton and its contained children.
After GRN/QC, WMS creates putaway tasks for accepted stock in receiving/staging.
Putaway engine identifies the handling level: Master Carton, Inner Carton or Loose Product/Serial.
System recommends destination zone/bin using product category, model, customer ownership, condition, capacity and storage rules.
Operator opens Putaway Task on handheld/mobile device.
Operator scans the source/staging location.
Operator scans the Master Carton or Inner Carton barcode. For loose serialized stock, scan product serial/IMEI.
System validates that the handling unit is in the expected source location and is eligible for putaway.
If a Master Carton is scanned and remains sealed, all contained Inner Cartons and product/serials move logically with the Master Carton.
If an Inner Carton is scanned independently, WMS validates that it has been removed/broken from its parent Master Carton before independent movement.
Operator scans the destination bin/location.
System validates bin capacity, permitted product/category/customer/condition and mixing rules.
Operator confirms putaway.
WMS creates movement Receiving/Staging -> Storage Bin and updates the current location for the handling unit and all contained inventory.
Putaway task becomes COMPLETED. Partial putaway remains OPEN/PARTIAL until all expected handling units are completed.
Any mismatch is routed to Putaway Exception with reason, supervisor action and audit trail.
24.1 Putaway Handling Rules
Scenario
Required WMS Behaviour
Full Master Carton Putaway
Scan MC once; move MC + all child Inner Cartons + contained products/serials to destination bin.
Inner Carton Putaway
Parent MC must be opened/broken or hierarchy updated; scan IC; move only that IC and its contents.
Loose Serialized Putaway
Serial must no longer be locked inside an intact carton; scan each serial or approved batch method.
Partial Master Carton
Record carton break; remaining children stay linked to parent/current location while removed children become independently movable.
Mixed Destination
One master carton cannot simultaneously exist in multiple bins. Break carton before distributing inner cartons.
Damaged Carton
Move to QC/quarantine; do not complete normal available-stock putaway.
Wrong Bin
Block confirmation and show permitted/recommended bin.
Duplicate Scan
Do not create a second movement; show already-putaway location/status.

24.3 Suggested Putaway Location Logic
Product/SKU storage strategy and compatible zone.
Customer-owned versus common stock segregation.
Serialized/high-value/security-controlled storage requirements.
Product category, model and condition.
Available bin capacity and carton dimensions/weight when maintained.
FIFO/FEFO or receipt-date strategy where applicable.
Avoid unnecessary carton breaking by preferring bins that can hold the complete Master Carton.
Do not mix incompatible products/customers/statuses when bin-mixing rules prohibit it.
Quarantine, damaged, repair and available inventory must remain in their permitted zones.
25. Carton Break / Repack Process
User selects Break Carton/Repack transaction and scans the Master Carton or Inner Carton.
System displays the complete expected contents.
User selects/scans the Inner Cartons or product units being removed.
For serialized products, removed serials are explicitly scanned when required.
WMS validates that removed quantity plus remaining quantity equals the original carton content.
Parent/child hierarchy is updated without losing historical relationship.
New handling-unit barcode may be generated for repacked stock.
Carton break/repack event is audited with user, time, source location and reason.
Only after hierarchy update can removed Inner Cartons/products be put away or transferred independently.
26. Outbound Impact of Carton Hierarchy
Picking should support full Master Carton, full Inner Carton or loose-unit allocation depending on order quantity.
Full-carton picks should avoid unnecessary serial-by-serial handling while still retaining serial traceability.
If an order requires less than a full carton, WMS creates/records a carton-break operation before loose/inner-carton picking.
Dispatch hierarchy must preserve which Master/Inner Carton contains each serialized asset.
Destination receiving can scan the outer handling unit and validate child contents according to configured trust/scan policy.
27. Revised End-to-End WMS Flow
Product Master & Packaging Configuration -> ASN -> Master Carton Receipt -> Inner Carton Receipt -> Product/Serial Validation -> GRN -> Inbound QC -> Receiving/Staging -> Carton-Aware Putaway -> Storage Inventory -> Reservation -> Picking -> Carton Break if Required -> Packing -> Dispatch -> Destination ASN/Receipt -> Region/Hub Inventory -> FSE Issue -> Customer Installation/Service -> Replacement/De-installation -> Reverse Logistics -> Warehouse/Repair/Refurbish/Scrap.
28. Additional Acceptance Criteria - Inbound and Putaway
A Master Carton barcode cannot be received twice against the same or another active receipt without authorized exception handling.
Every Inner Carton has a valid parent Master Carton unless it has been formally broken/repacked into an independent handling unit.
For serialized products, each serial is unique and traceable to Product -> Inner Carton -> Master Carton -> ASN -> GRN.
Putting away a sealed Master Carton updates the location of all contained inventory without creating duplicate child movements.
An Inner Carton cannot be moved independently while the system still treats its parent Master Carton as sealed/intact.
The same Master Carton cannot exist in two warehouse bins.
Partial putaway retains accurate remaining quantity/handling units at staging.
Wrong-bin, wrong-product, wrong-carton, duplicate-serial and quantity-variance conditions are blocked or routed to authorized exception workflow.
Carton break/repack preserves complete historical traceability.
Outbound, transfer and reverse-logistics modules consume the same handling-unit hierarchy and movement ledger.
